How no-cost EMI works

Your bank charges interest on the EMI, same as any loan. To make it "no cost", the shop takes that interest off the price upfront, so your instalments add up to the sticker price. But you still pay 18% GST on the interest the bank charged, and most banks add a processing fee, with GST on that too. Nobody discounts the GST.

Also check whether the sale's instant bank discount still applies when you pay by EMI. If it doesn't, losing it can cost more than the GST and the fee put together.
