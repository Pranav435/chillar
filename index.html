What it does, by itself

  1. Unticks add-ons you didn't ask for

    Pre-ticked travel insurance, donations, tips, protection plans, zero-cancellation add-ons and "send me offers" boxes get unticked as the page loads. Discounts, coupons and the terms box are left alone, and anything you tick yourself stays ticked.

    "Chillar unticked 2 add-ons: travel insurance for INR 249, donate INR 5. That keeps INR 254 in your pocket. Undo?"

  2. Points out the fees hiding in your total

    Platform fee, convenience fee, packaging, handling: Chillar adds them up on checkout pages and tells you what share of your bill they are.

    "Fees on this page: Platform fee INR 10, Packaging INR 25. That's INR 35 out of your INR 385 total (9 percent)."

  3. Reminds you before free trials charge you

    When you start a free trial, Chillar notes when it ends and reminds you 2 days before, with a one-click Google Calendar reminder.

    "Looks like you're starting a free trial. It ends around 10 Nov, then INR 649 a month. I'll remind you on 8 Nov."

A small counter keeps track of how much it has saved you.

Private by design

Everything runs on your laptop. Chillar has no server and no account, and nothing you browse is sent anywhere.

Install in a minute

  1. Buy Chillar and download the zip, then unzip it. Keep the Chillar folder somewhere safe, like Documents.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Press Load unpacked and choose the Chillar folder. Done.
